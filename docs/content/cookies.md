This policy covers the spec17.dev website. See also the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

## Your choice

Google Analytics is optional. We load Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager only after you select **Accept**. If you reject analytics on your first visit or leave the choice open, neither service loads. You can read the whole site either way. We do not turn on advertising storage, advertising user data or ad personalisation.

To change your choice, select **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page. When you withdraw consent, the site deletes the Google Analytics cookies and reloads. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before it, or delete data already sent to Google Analytics.

## Necessary storage

The site keeps these values in your browser's local storage. If your rejection of analytics cannot be saved there, the site keeps `spec17.consent` in session storage instead, until you close the tab. They are not cookies, and the site does not send them to a server.

| Name | Purpose | Kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `spec17.consent` | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics, and when | The site honours your choice for 365 days, then asks again on your next visit. The old entry stays until replaced or cleared. |
| `spec17.theme` | Remembers the light or dark theme, if you pick one | Until you pick the system theme or clear site data |

Cloudflare may also set a security cookie if it challenges a request. For example, `cf_clearance` lets a visitor who passes a check continue browsing without immediately repeating it. [Cloudflare’s cookie reference](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/)

## Analytics cookies

These are set only after you accept.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells visitors apart | After up to 2 years |
| `_ga_GQXY8SX524` | Google Analytics | Keeps the state of a visit | After up to 2 years |

By default, Google renews the expiry on each visit while analytics is enabled. Your browser may expire the cookies sooner. Google describes them in [its cookie usage guide](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207).
