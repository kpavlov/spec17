## Who receives it

- **Google Ireland Limited** provides Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. The site loads these services only with your consent. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Your browser still connects to Google, which therefore sees your IP address. See [how Google uses data from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Cloudflare** receives your browser’s request, including your IP address, to deliver cached pages, forward requests to GitHub Pages and protect the site. [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site and handles requests forwarded by Cloudflare. GitHub records IP addresses for security. [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
- **Our email provider** handles your address and message if you email us.

### International transfers

Cloudflare, GitHub and Google may process personal data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For transfers to US recipients covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the European Commission has recognised that the framework provides adequate protection. Where no adequacy decision covers a transfer, the providers’ terms allow for the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. You can email [info@itstaff.ee](mailto:info@itstaff.ee) for a copy of the safeguards that apply to your data. See [Cloudflare’s data processing addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/), [GitHub’s international transfer terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement#international-data-transfers) and [Google’s transfer frameworks](https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks).

## How long we keep it

- Google Analytics event data has a **2-month** retention setting. User data has a **14-month** setting, which Google also applies to events marked as key events. Each new event restarts the 14-month clock for the user identifier. It does not restart the clock for previous events. Google deletes expired data in its monthly deletion cycle. Standard aggregated reports can be kept longer. [Google Analytics retention rules](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196)
- Cookies and browser storage: see the [cookie policy](../cookies/).
- Delivery and security logs are retained by Cloudflare and GitHub for as long as needed to operate and secure their services and meet legal obligations. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and [GitHub’s retention policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement#security-and-retention).
- Email: as long as we need it to deal with your message and to meet legal duties.

## Your rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal data. Where the legal conditions are met, you can also ask us to erase it, restrict its use, or provide data you gave us in a portable format. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time under **Cookie settings** in the footer. This leaves the lawfulness of earlier processing unaffected.

### Your right to object

You can object at any time, for reasons relating to your situation, to processing based on our legitimate interests. Email [info@itstaff.ee](mailto:info@itstaff.ee).

### Requests and complaints

To exercise any of these rights, email [info@itstaff.ee](mailto:info@itstaff.ee). We respond within one month. If the complexity or number of requests requires more time, we will explain why within that first month. The extension can be up to two further months.

You can complain to the [Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate](https://www.aki.ee/en) or to a supervisory authority where you live, work or believe an infringement took place.
